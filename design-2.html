How it works

  1. 1

    Install once

    Add Prompticon from the Chrome Web Store. It wakes up inside your AI chats and nowhere else, with a three-step onboarding to show you the ropes.

  2. 2

    Spot the toolbar

    Your replies float above the composer. Drag them anywhere, collapse them to a pill, reset with one click. Prompticon remembers a position per site.

  3. 3

    Reply at click speed

    Click to fill the composer for review, flip a switch to send on click, hold a chip for its long-press variant, or go hands-off with Alt+1 to 9.
