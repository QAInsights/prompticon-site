In control, by design

  1. 01

    Drags where you want

    Grab the handle and drop the toolbar anywhere. Prompticon remembers a separate position for every site, and one click on ↺ snaps it home above the composer.

  2. 02

    Eight profiles, one click apart

    General, Developer, Writer, Student, Support, Recruiter, Sales and Quiz. Start from a curated pack, then edit, trim or add replies until it fits your workflow.

  3. 03

    Templates that ask back

    Save replies like "Explain {{topic}} for {{audience}}". Prompticon asks for the values, then inserts or sends the finished prompt.

  4. 04

    Long-press for the long form

    Hold Yes and it becomes "Yes, but explain why." Every chip can carry an expanded follow-up for the moments a single word is not enough.

  5. 05

    Keyboard-first

    Alt + 1..9 fires your first nine replies without touching the mouse. Alt + P opens a searchable command palette.

  6. 06

    Smart detection, opt-in

    Turn on local-only detection and the latest AI response surfaces temporary Yes/No, True/False or A-E answer chips. Off by default, always your call.

Quietly useful, everywhere

Nothing leaves your browser.

No analytics, no trackers, no network calls. Your replies and settings live in chrome.storage on your machine, and the only permission Prompticon asks for is storage. That is the entire privacy story.
