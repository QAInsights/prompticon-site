01 Overview

Every AI conversation repeats itself: yes, no, continue, shorter, explain again. Prompticon keeps your best replies on a floating toolbar above the composer, so they cost one click instead of ten keystrokes. Drag it, collapse it, template it, shortcut it. It is your toolbar, in your browser, under your control.

02 Capability index

C-01

Floating toolbar

Draggable, collapsible, with per-site position memory and a one-click reset to the composer.

C-02

Reply profiles

General, Developer, Writer, Student, Support, Recruiter, Sales and Quiz packs. Every reply editable, deletable, addable.

C-03

Click behavior

Fill the composer for review (default) or send immediately. Your choice, one toggle.

C-04

Long-press variants

Hold a chip to insert its expanded follow-up: hold Yes for "Yes, but explain why."

C-05

Template variables

Replies like "Explain {{topic}} for {{audience}}" prompt for values, then compose the finished prompt.

C-06

Keyboard control

Alt+1..9 fires the first nine replies. Alt+P opens a searchable command palette with arrow-key selection.

C-07

Smart detection

Opt-in, local-only. Surfaces Yes/No, True/False and A-E answer chips from the latest AI response.

C-08

Site visibility

Show the toolbar everywhere, or choose exactly which supported websites get it.

05 Privacy record

The entire data pipeline is your machine.

Prompticon has no backend, no analytics SDK, no third-party tracker and no network call to its own name. Replies, profiles and settings persist in chrome.storage.local, and smart detection runs only if you switch it on.
